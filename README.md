# Wingman

Your Google Calendar and Gmail on Even Realities G2 smart glasses, with Gemini to draft replies and calendar invites.

The project is being built step by step. Planned pieces:

1. **Calendar**: show today's and upcoming Google Calendar events on the glasses.
2. **Email**: show new and important Gmail messages as short summaries.
3. **Drafting**: use Gemini to draft email replies and calendar invites, then review and send them from the glasses.
4. **Glasses**: an [Even Hub](https://hub.evenrealities.com/docs/get-started/overview) app that renders all of this on the G2, controlled with the R1 ring.

Nothing is sent without your confirmation.
